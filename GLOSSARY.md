# Asymmetri

## Virtual Worlds

**Campus edition**

The version of a virtual campus a visitor explores, such as Regular, Halloween
or Christmas. A seasonal edition adds its own scenery and sound to the campus.

**Automatic**

The edition choice that follows the real campus's local calendar. Outside a
scheduled holiday, the visitor explores the Regular edition.

**Seasonal replay**

A visitor's deliberate choice to explore a holiday edition outside its scheduled
dates for one visit. When the replay ends, the visitor's saved Regular or
Automatic choice applies again, subject to its expiry on the campus calendar.

**Campus visit**

One stay in a virtual campus. A visit continues through a tab switch, phone
lock or midnight and ends when the visitor leaves the campus, closes the tab
or reloads. Opening the campus in another tab starts a separate visit.

**Live atmosphere**

The light and sky at the campus's actual local time, with weather drawn from a
recent observation at a named nearby station. Local time and sunlight continue
when weather is unavailable.

**Simulated atmosphere**

A visitor-selected hour, season or weather for the current visit. Conditions
staged in Photo stay in effect when walking resumes, until the visitor selects
Live or starts another visit.
