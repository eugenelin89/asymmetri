# CWRU source and asset notices

Original application and procedural geometry are MIT licensed; see LICENSE.
The explorer adapts the Asymmetri UBC project and uses the repository's Three.js,
React and Next/Vinext dependencies under their existing licences.

Geography is derived from ©OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database
License1.0. See https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and the pinned public
`cwru/v0.1-2026-10-31/manifest.json` for request URLs, hashes and transformation
notes. Distributed derived data retains that licence and attribution.

Official university descriptions, public floor plans and photographs inform
original architectural interpretations. They remain linked references, not
redistributed textures or plan images. `architecture-sources.json` records the
specific sources and the limits of each reconstruction.

Public university biographies, athletics rosters and publicly indexed LinkedIn
education excerpts supply limited factual character information. Their wording,
portraits, likenesses and voices are not reproduced as assets. Each record in
`data/people.json` identifies access limitations. Identity, enrollment and live
whereabouts are not verified by the game.

Halloween models are original procedural costume geometry. Comic/manga costumes
are original genre-inspired designs. The requested Friday-the-13th-inspired
hockey-mask pursuer is an unofficial fan interpretation; no film stills, logos,
licensed models, audio or other external character assets are distributed.

The Halloween music, howls, cries and screams are original Web Audio synthesis.
No recorded soundtrack, film audio, sampled human voice or external audio file is used.
