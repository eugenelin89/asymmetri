## Buildings and people

Stanford campus maps, official building/venue descriptions and public photos are linked in `architecture-sources.json`; they are references, not redistributed imagery. Museum artworks and photographs are not copied. Interiors are original interpretations. Temporary real-world public access limits are documented independently of virtual access.

Faculty/professional facts come from official Stanford profiles and departments. Athlete identities, sport, jersey and class/position facts are factual extracts from the official Stanford Athletics season rosters; public stable IDs deduplicate multiple team listings. Roster pages and source dates appear in `data/roster-coverage.json`. No athlete portraits, biographies or promotional text are copied. Student records use public academic profile references, public LinkedIn education excerpts and supplied research. Individually inspected sources, uninspected supplied references and access limitations are distinguished in `data/people-sources.json` and `data/student-import-verification.json`; enrollment is not registrar-verified. No inaccessible/private fields or photos are included.

Educational character dialogue is original, bounded text. It is not a real person's speech, endorsement or whereabouts. Avatars are generic, original geometry, not facial likeness reconstruction. Self-submitted community content remains participant-controlled through the profile ownership and report interfaces.
