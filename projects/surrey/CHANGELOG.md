# Surrey release history

## V0.6 — Open Roads (candidate)

17.0 km of connected source-supported road paths, eight roadside destinations,
A* GPS with rerouting/arrival/clear route, complete Case05 archive detour,
expanded bounded traffic and fictional signals, safe stop rejoining, schema5
migration and independent simultaneous touch/keyboard holds. Compact mobile HUD
retained; map navigation is an explicit scrollable overlay. One verified compact
and animated rail remain; extra vehicle footprints and boarding are deferred.
Rendered-browser acceptance is blocked; this candidate is not a deployed release.

## V0.5 — On the Move

Enterable compact car; bounded acceleration, steering, brake/reverse and full-body
swept road collision; safe exit/recovery; chase camera; compact held touch pedals
and km/h display. Complete Jules→car→Quinn projector delivery pays $55 once.
A validated Central Avenue / City Parkway snapshot powers route guidance and a
small yielding traffic loop. Original train cars move on one connected elevated
chain; side platforms and lights improve Surrey Central, without boarding.
Schema3→4 migration preserves earlier cases and settings; car reload stops safely.
Browser driving exposed and fixed dense rail-column camera obstruction. GPU teardown
now includes route/track lines as well as shared meshes. Old snapshots remain intact.

## V0.4 — Living Surrey

Saved day/night cycle and stable visibility; timed pedestrian roles and activity;
night window/lamp/shop lighting; illustrative entrance/rack/planter detail; original
opt-in performer audio; complete Missing Person case with two approaches and endings;
authored encounter variants, evening mature eligibility and useful Jo trust; explicit
schema2→3 migration; safer camera smoothing and compact phone safe-area spacing.
Terrain remains flat after fresh official metadata review. Existing snapshots retained.

## V0.3 — City Reconstructed

Official road surfaces, active curbs/medians/paths, metre-verified matched building
heights, original landmark/facade detail, persistent view controls and compact phones.

## V0.2 — Dangerous Streets

Second complete case, six fictional street encounters, aerial exploration and saves.

## V0.1 — Surrey Adventure

Initial source-backed city, backpack mission, community help and optional pickpocket.
