# Asymmetrico site strategy

## Site narrative

The website is one connected argument:

1. sports-development tools are unevenly distributed;
2. fragmented information makes feedback temporary and athletes easier to reduce to
   snapshots;
3. asymmetric advantage comes from leverage, not imitation of scale;
4. Asymmetrico Platform proves that sophisticated development infrastructure can
   work in a real community environment;
5. multiple perspectives create a richer picture without becoming objective truth;
6. feedback should accumulate across seasons;
7. accessible engineering accounts for limited time, budgets, hardware, and
   changing teams;
8. related sensor and biomechanics research extends the same accessibility thesis;
9. technology matters only when it creates clearer goals, stronger conversations,
   continuity, visibility, and opportunity.

## Page architecture

- `/` — full brand narrative and primary calls to action
- `/work` — scalable portfolio separating deployed and emerging work
- `/work/asymmetrico-platform` — detailed, privacy-conscious product case study
- `/why-asymmetrico` — asymmetric-advantage explanation
- `/about` — engineering origin, working method, and current sports focus
- `/contact` — direct email contact without unnecessary data collection

## Product hierarchy

### Deployed

Asymmetrico Platform: structured evaluations, multiple perspectives, athlete
profiles, timelines, comparison, review, completion tracking, reporting,
role-aware accounts, and seasonal context.

### Emerging

Pitching biomechanics, affordable inertial sensing, motion-signal analysis, temporal
attention research, and physical-system prototyping.

### Future

Video-derived information, cross-source athlete profiles, longitudinal performance
data, intelligent development support, and broader sports.

## Design principles

- Use deliberate imbalance through scale, offset, and negative space.
- Return to symmetry when structure or comparison requires it.
- Alternate Ink and Canvas chapters for narrative pacing.
- Use Clay for intervention, Signal Green for progress, and Data Blue for
  information.
- Use authentic photography only where it adds evidence or human stakes.
- Keep the product interface calmer than the editorial site.
- Make every diagram understandable in text.
- Preserve the story on mobile through sequence and scale rather than compressed
  desktop grids.

## Content architecture

Verified public facts, navigation, metadata, image descriptions, contact details,
principles, perspectives, product capabilities, research disclosures, and core
messages are typed and centralized in `content/site.ts`.

Page files own sequence and route-specific explanation. Reusable visuals own
presentation only.

## Implementation plan

1. Audit the existing site and both reference repositories.
2. Verify product capabilities in models, services, permissions, routes, templates,
   tests, and architecture documentation.
3. Select only first-party, publicly suitable images; recreate the platform interface
   with synthetic data.
4. Replace the former identity and positioning with a six-route editorial system.
5. Add the logo family, metadata, icons, social card, structured data, sitemap, and
   robots rules.
6. Validate accessibility, responsive behavior, content accuracy, privacy, prohibited
   references, linting, types, and production output.

## Attribution decisions

The public site does not attribute the deployed product to its originating sports
organization. It describes the environment accurately and anonymously.

Related biomechanics and physical-system material is described as research
foundation or emerging work, not as a mature Asymmetrico commercial product.
Internal provenance is recorded in `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

## Privacy constraints

- no real athlete names or histories;
- no direct source-application screenshots;
- no evaluation responses or private coach notes;
- no team, division, account, deployment, or repository identifiers;
- no credentials or public application link;
- synthetic names in every platform visual;
- no contact form or visitor-data collection.

## Asset gaps and facts requiring confirmation

- The operating public domain and contact mailbox are inherited from the prior
  company site and should be reconfirmed before a domain migration.
- Direct rights documentation for reference photography is not stored in this
  repository. Reuse is based on first-party public publication and remains subject to
  repository-owner confirmation.
- No current product screenshots met the combined privacy, anonymity, and branding
  requirements; the site uses a faithful reconstruction.
- No customer, usage, adoption, outcome, certification, or commercial-performance
  claims are made.

## Recommended future content

- verified founder or team biographies;
- a confirmed legal company address only if intentionally public;
- product screenshots prepared from a dedicated synthetic environment;
- additional deployed work only after verification;
- explicit research attribution and publication links when ownership and authorship
  are confirmed.
